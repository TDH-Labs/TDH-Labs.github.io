TDH Labs ("TDH Labs", "we", "us") builds custom AI workflow automation, dedicated private infrastructure, and ongoing site reliability services for business clients. We share personal information only with service providers essential to delivering our services — including Stripe (payments), Twilio (messaging), and GitHub (code hosting). Our AI automation runs on our own private infrastructure; client data is not used to train third-party foundation models.

We apply industry-standard safeguards (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls). We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.
